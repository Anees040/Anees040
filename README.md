## 🔭 Product Roadmap

> These are **planned ideas**, not completed projects. They will move into the project sections only after real implementation begins or a project is shipped.

### 🛍️ ShopMate AI — Planned / Next Build
**AI WhatsApp sales agent for Pakistani Shopify stores**

An AI sales agent designed to handle customer conversations in **Urdu, English, and Roman Urdu**, including text and voice notes. The planned product connects to Shopify to answer product questions, send product images, negotiate within owner-defined discount limits, confirm COD orders, and revive silent customers.

**Product focus:** WhatsApp · Shopify · voice notes · COD/RTO reduction · controlled negotiation · seller analytics

### 🔬 HealthLens — Planned
**Making medical test reports easier to understand**

A concept for an AI-assisted mobile experience where a user photographs a medical test report and receives a clearer, easier-to-understand explanation of the information in the report.

> Planned as an **information/explanation tool**, not a diagnostic or replacement-for-a-doctor system.

### 🧠 MindGym — Future Concept
**“ChatGPT does your thinking. MindGym rebuilds it.”**

A learning app designed around productive struggle. Instead of immediately giving users answers to programming, math, or logic problems, the AI would guide them through **Socratic questions and progressive hints**, then ask them to explain their solution in their own words.

**Core idea:** problem solving · Socratic hints · independence tracking · explain-back · programming & aptitude practice
